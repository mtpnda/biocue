BioCue for Wear OS delivers gentle haptic cues and emotion check-ins directly on your wrist to build habit awareness, stress resilience, and everyday mindfulness.

FEATURES:
• Gentle Haptic Cueing: Subtle, non-intrusive wrist vibrations that guide attention without drawing unwanted social attention.
• One-Tap Emotion Logging: Log emotional states instantly from your watch Tile or watch face complication.
• Standalone & Companion Modes: Works independently for continuous sensing and syncs with your companion Android device when connected.
• Efficient Sensor Integration: Powered by Wear OS Health Services for efficient passive data collection with minimal battery impact.
• Emergency Signal Routing: Quick access to send distress or status updates to your private Circle.

REQUIREMENTS:
• Wear OS 3.0 or higher.
