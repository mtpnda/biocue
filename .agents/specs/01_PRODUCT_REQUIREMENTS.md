# 01. Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Objective
BioCue is a multi-device Android application (Phone + Wear OS) designed to predict and detect sensory overload meltdowns in autistic individuals. 

## Target Audience
- **The Wearer:** An autistic individual wearing a Wear OS 3+ smartwatch (e.g., Galaxy Watch 4). Runs the watch app and the mobile app on their own phone.
- **The Caregiver:** A parent, teacher, or support worker running the mobile app on their own separate phone, paired to a wearer's account.

## Account Model
- **2-in-1 (amended):** one account can be a **Wearer and a Caregiver at once**. A wearer registers **up to 2 caregivers** who can observe them; a caregiver observes the wearer they are paired to. The two are different people's *data*, never mixed.
- Modes (WEARER, CAREGIVER) are stored in Firestore; a user may hold either or both.
- **Pairing**: Caregiver connects to a wearer via a 6-digit numeric code generated by the wearer. The wearer must explicitly approve each connection.
- **Limits**: One wearer can have at most **2 approved caregivers**. The wearer can remove any caregiver at any time.
- **On removal**: The caregiver immediately loses all read access. Their local cache of wearer data is wiped. Firestore security rules block further reads.

## Data Flow
- **Watch → Wearer Phone**: Google Play Services Wearable DataLayer API (Bluetooth/Wi-Fi, local only).
- **Wearer Phone → Firestore**: Event-driven sync of stress events only. Current status (stress %, activity, tier) stays on the wearer's phone (see 07).
- **Firestore → Caregiver Phone**: Real-time snapshot listeners (read-only).
- **Watch ↛ Caregiver**: The watch never communicates directly with the caregiver's device.

## Core Philosophy
1. **Zero-Anxiety UX:** The watch interface must never display live vitals (heart rate numbers) or chaotic animations, which can induce stress. It acts quietly in the background with a single "I need help" SOS button.
2. **Extreme Privacy:** All physiological data (HR, HRV, accelerometer) is stored on-device in the wearer's local Room database. Only derived data (stress events, activity labels, stress level) is synced to Firestore for caregiver visibility. Raw sensor windows never leave the wearer's phone.
3. **Consent & Battery:** Continuous background GPS tracking is explicitly forbidden. GPS is only fetched via an on-demand "SOS Ping" during a severe meltdown.
4. **Wearer Owns Data:** The wearer has full control over who can see their data. Caregivers can be added and removed at any time. Removal is immediate and irreversible — the caregiver loses all access and local data is wiped.

## Success Metrics
- Useful early warning for ≥50% of episodes within 90 days.
- Watch battery life ≥16 hours (full waking day).
- Caregiver receives stress alerts within 30 seconds of a Tier 2 meltdown.
- Pairing flow completes in <60 seconds.
